# Relational-shape foundation

This track contains the original theorem: appending nonnegative distinguishing
coordinates to an additive distance produces nested, nonincreasing
fixed-radius neighborhoods. The theorem is mathematical and contains no
cosmological identification or dynamics.

The standalone companion note
[Finite Observation and Broader Physical Processes](finite_observation/finite_observation.pdf)
develops geometric and statistical observability bounds, a coupled mechanical
example, and a conditional probability law for further process scales.
Its [source, full proofs, and reproduction instructions](finite_observation/README.md)
are in `finite_observation/`.

- `relational_shape.tex` — source
- `relational_shape.pdf` — compiled paper
