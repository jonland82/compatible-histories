03 The distance grows. The ratio settles.

Every new coordinate adds evidence. Finite records fluctuate; the limit is almost sure.

Measured R² / nPredicted r

“Almost surely” means convergence with probability one as the record grows without bound.

WHAT IS BEING RECORDED?

Independent observations, retained.

At each coordinate, each constituent is 1 with probability \(p\), otherwise 0. A centroid is the average of \(m\) constituents. Its history keeps every past average; the amplitude \(a=1\) is fixed.

\(C_i^{(n)}=\left(\frac{a}{m}\sum_{\ell=1}^{m}B_{i\ell t}\right)_{t=1}^{n}\)
WHAT DOES THE 3D VIEW PRESERVE?

Exact distances from the reference.

Each dot is one cluster’s centroid history. Its distance from C₁ uses all n coordinates. Directions are assigned for readability; distances between other clusters are not preserved. The receding dots retain earlier radii. The spheres and plot axes use the same fixed scale throughout a run.

\(R_n=\min_{j\ne1}\|C_1^{(n)}-C_j^{(n)}\|\)
WHAT CAN WE INFER?

A density first. Then the whole record.

Clip the measured ratio to \([0,a^2/(2m)]\) to get \(\widehat r_n\). It determines binary entropy \(\widehat h_n\). Multiplying by \(Kmn\) estimates the record’s total entropy.

\(\widehat h_n=h_2\!\left(\frac{1-\sqrt{1-2m\widehat r_n/a^2}}{2}\right)\)
