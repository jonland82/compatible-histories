Invariant Measures on Compatible Classical Histories

A deterministic trajectory is not a product of independent snapshots. It is one physical initial condition carried through dynamics. That observation turns invariant phase-space measure into a measure on entire histories—and makes canonical, temporal, and gauge invariance testable.

The construction in one sentence

Reduce to physical initial data, push their invariant measure through deterministic dynamics, and ask what fraction of complete histories remains compatible with the record.

The conceptual move

Solution space is another representation of physical initial-data space

Paper 3 found invariant measure on states but left a temporal divergence in raw path counts. Determinism removes the apparent infinity of independent slice choices.

01 · Reduce

Keep physical degrees of freedom

Start on reduced phase space $\Gamma_{\rm phys}$ after constraints and gauge redundancy have been removed. Normalize a physical ensemble $\nu_0$ there.

02 · Evolve

Map data to solutions

Under suitable existence and uniqueness conditions, every initial datum $z$ determines one complete classical history $\gamma_z$.

03 · Push forward

Measure each history once

A set of histories receives exactly the measure of the initial conditions that generate it. No product over temporal coordinate cells is needed.

Physical prior

$$ d\nu_0(z)=f_0(z)\,d\mu_L(z), \qquad \int_{\Gamma_{\rm phys}}d\nu_0=1. $$

Liouville measure supplies invariant volume. The ensemble density $f_0$ remains physical input; invariance does not choose nature’s prior.

History pushforward

$$ F:\Gamma_{\rm phys}\to\mathcal H, \quad z\mapsto\gamma_z, \qquad \boxed{\mu_{\mathcal H}=F_*\nu_0}. $$

For a history event $B$, $\mu_{\mathcal H}(B)=\nu_0(F^{-1}(B))$. Canonical invariance is inherited from the physical measure.

From measure to information

Let the physical record weight complete histories

A hard record can select a subset; a finite-resolution record can grade histories continuously. The distinction separates surviving volume from posterior information.

Surviving measure

$$ 0\le W_t(\gamma)\le1, \qquad Z_t=\int_{\mathcal H}W_t(\gamma)\,d\mu_{\mathcal H}(\gamma), $$ $$ C_{\rm surv}(t)=-\log_2Z_t. $$

If later records never increase a history’s compatibility, $C_{\rm surv}$ is nondecreasing—the measure-theoretic version of Paper 1’s nested neighborhoods.

Posterior information

$$ d\mu_t=\frac{W_t}{Z_t}\,d\mu_{\mathcal H}, \qquad 0\le C_{\rm KL}(t)\le C_{\rm surv}(t). $$

For hard compatibility the two coincide. For soft weights they differ: remaining prior volume is not the same object as information gained by an observer.

Three invariance gates

The same physical history must not acquire false bits from its description

The construction is tested against changes of clock, nonlinear canonical coordinates, and nonlinear gauge surfaces. Each test includes a controlled failure showing the necessary Jacobian.

Clock reparameterization

For $t=f(\tau)$, include the lapse $N(\tau)=dt/d\tau$:

$$ W_T=\exp\!\left[-\frac1{2\sigma^2}\int d\tau\,N(\tau) (x_\gamma(f(\tau))-y(f(\tau)))^2\right]. $$

The lapse makes the exponent an integral over physical time rather than a count of coordinate slices.

Gauge-fixed physical measure

$$ d\mu_{\rm phys}^{(\chi)} =\delta(\mathcal C)\,\delta(\chi) |\{\chi,\mathcal C\}|\,dx\,dp\,dt\,dp_t. $$

The Faddeev–Popov determinant compensates for how a gauge surface intersects physical orbits. Without it, gauge choice becomes false history information.

What these tests cover

The result establishes a finite deterministic classical construction in deparameterizable, single-constraint systems. It does not yet cover multiple gravitational constraints, spacetime diffeomorphisms, Gribov ambiguities, or quantum interference.

The completed classical object

The series now has a compact invariant candidate

Each earlier correction appears in one measurement hierarchy.

Invariant compatible-history information

$$ \boxed{ C_{\rm surv}(t) =-\log_2\!\int_{\Gamma_{\rm phys}} W_t(\gamma_z)\,d\nu_0(z)}. $$

Reduce physical initial data; push their measure through dynamics; weight complete histories with a gauge-invariant, physically timed compatibility functional.

The cosmological hypothesis remains

$$ H_{\rm rel}(\tau) =\frac{\ln2}{3}\frac{dC_{\rm surv}}{d\tau}. $$

This paper strengthens the definition of $C_{\rm surv}$. It does not derive this constitutive relation from gravity or show that the information rate equals the observed Hubble rate.

Three structures still needed for cosmology

  • A reduced or gauge-fixed measure for the Hamiltonian and momentum constraints of general relativity.
  • A causal, relational record defined without coordinate time or coordinate position.
  • A prior on gravitational initial data specified independently of the expansion history it will be asked to predict.

The next scientific gate

Take the invariant object into a finite gravitational system

A minisuperspace model or constrained cosmological perturbation system should first pass canonical, clock, gauge, and refinement tests. Only then should its independently defined history-information rate be compared with held-out expansion data.
