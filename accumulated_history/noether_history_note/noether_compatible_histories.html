The correction in one sentence

Symmetry determines which roads are physically admissible; invariant phase volume determines how much room those roads occupy.

Physical admissibility

Conservation replaces adjustable proximity

Noether’s connection between time-translation symmetry and energy conservation selects allowed transitions before anything is counted.

Energy-shell cells

$$ \mathcal C_{\epsilon}(E_0) =\{\text{phase cells whose }H\text{-range contains }E_0\}. $$

The effective energy uncertainty comes from exact cell intersection, not a tolerance tuned to a desired bit rate.

Constraint codimension

$$ C_\epsilon =\log_2\frac1\epsilon-\log_2\frac{A_{\rm grid}}V+o(1). $$

For one smooth constraint, each halving of cell width adds one raw bit. That divergence is the geometric signature of codimension one.

From states to stationary-action histories

$$ R_m=x_{m+1}+(-2+\Delta t^2)x_m+x_{m-1}=0. $$

Interval arithmetic admits a candidate continuation only when its cells can contain a discrete Euler–Lagrange solution. Spatial refinement exposes codimension; temporal refinement exposes the density of imposed slices. Neither is yet an invariant history measure.

The invariance test

Convergence is not enough; the answer must survive canonical coordinates

The codimension-subtracted cell count converges but changes under equal-area grid deformations and a canonical shear. Liouville phase volume does not.

Canonical phase measure

$$ d\Gamma=dx\,dp, \qquad dx\wedge dp=dQ\wedge dP. $$

Hamiltonian flow and canonical transformations preserve Liouville measure.

Energy-band density

$$ g_{\tau_E}(E)=\frac1{2\tau_E} \int\mathbf1\{|H-E|\le\tau_E\}\,d\Gamma. $$

Unlike cell incidence, the integral weights each region by phase volume. As $\tau_E\to0$, it approaches the microcanonical density of states.

Scientific status

The measurement hierarchy is validated in finite classical tests. It does not yet provide a covariant measure on complete histories, a continuum time normalization, or the constrained symplectic measure for gravity. The Hubble-scale bit rate is now a target, not a calibration.
